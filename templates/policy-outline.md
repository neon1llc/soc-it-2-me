---

Every policy opens with the same header block:

> **Owner:** name and role · **Version:** 1.0 · **Approved by:** name, date · **Next review:** date

Keep each one to one to three pages. Write "we" and "you," and state rules, not tools. See [module 5](../course/05-policies.md).

## Information security

Purpose and scope · Roles: leadership, the security owner, system owners, everyone · How the other policies fit together · How exceptions are requested and approved · Consequences of breaking policy

## Acceptable use

Company devices and accounts · Personal devices for work · Data you may and may not move or share · Reporting a lost device or a suspicious message · Monitoring notice

## Access control

Least privilege and roles · Requesting and approving access · Two-step sign-in and single sign-on · Administrator and production access · Quarterly reviews · Removal at departure, within one business day · Service accounts and secrets

## Change management

What counts as a change · Review by someone other than the author · Testing and automated checks · Deploying through the pipeline · Separate environments and test data · Emergency changes

## Incident response

What an incident is · How to report one · Severity levels · Roles · Steps: detect, contain, fix, recover, review · Customer and legal notice · Evidence handling · Yearly tabletop

## Business continuity and backup

Critical systems and recovery targets · Backup frequency, retention, encryption, and separation · Yearly restore test · Loss of a key system, provider, or person · Yearly plan review

## Vendor management

Risk tiers · Review before signing · Yearly reviews for high-risk vendors · Contract terms · Complementary controls from vendor reports · Offboarding a vendor

## Data classification and retention

Classes (for example: public, internal, confidential, customer) · Handling rules for each · Retention periods · Deletion on request and at contract end · Secure disposal

## Risk management

Method and scoring scale · Yearly and on-change assessments · Treatments and acceptance · Ownership and review

## Human resources security

Background checks · Confidentiality agreements · Training at hire and yearly · Disciplinary process · Offboarding checklist

## Encryption and key management

Data in transit · Data at rest · Key storage, access, and rotation · Approved algorithms and protocols (named in a procedure, not the policy)
