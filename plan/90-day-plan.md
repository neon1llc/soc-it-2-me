---
title: The 90-day readiness plan
description: "Thirteen weeks from kickoff to a running control set, ready for a readiness assessment and the start of an observation window."
---

This plan assumes one owner with about eight hours a week, help from engineering and the people lead, and a company that already uses a cloud provider, an identity provider, and code review. If you're missing those basics, add a month.

| Weeks | Focus | Module | Done when |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Decide why and which report | [1](../course/01-decide-why.md) | Leadership approved the reason, the date, and the owner |
| 2 | Scope | [2](../course/02-scope.md) | Scope statement, system diagram, and vendor list drafted |
| 3 | Risk assessment | [3](../course/03-risk.md) | Register with fifteen or more risks, owners, and sign-off |
| 4 to 5 | Controls | [4](../course/04-controls.md) | Your own controls list, every criterion covered |
| 6 to 7 | Policies | [5](../course/05-policies.md) | Policy set approved and published, acknowledgments started |
| 8 | Evidence setup | [6](../course/06-evidence.md) | Tracker filled, logs and retention set, recurring tickets created |
| 9 to 10 | Close gaps | [4](../course/04-controls.md) | Every *start* control running, such as device management, training, or reviews |
| 11 | Run the calendar | [7](../course/07-operate.md) | First access review and tabletop done, calendar shared |
| 12 | Choose an auditor | [8](../course/08-the-audit.md) | Three firms interviewed, one engaged |
| 13 | Readiness check | [8](../course/08-the-audit.md) | Readiness assessment booked, or an internal walk-through of every control done |

## After week 13

- **Type I:** the auditor tests design as of a date soon after the readiness check.
- **Type II:** start the observation window once every control has run at least once. Keep the [calendar](observation-calendar.md) going, and expect fieldwork at the end of the window.

## Signs you're slipping

- The owner has missed two weekly check-ins.
- Controls are being written for what you plan to do "someday."
- Evidence lives in personal inboxes and chat threads.

If you see these, cut scope or move the date. Both are better than starting a window you can't finish.
