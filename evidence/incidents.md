---
title: "Evidence: incidents"
description: "Proof that an incident plan exists, incidents are handled and reviewed, and the plan is exercised each year."
---

Controls: INC-01 to INC-03.

## What the auditor asks for

- [ ] The approved incident response plan, with roles, severity levels, contact steps, and customer notice rules.
- [ ] The incident log for the window (the population), even if it's short.
- [ ] For each incident or a sample: timeline, actions, communication, closure, and lessons learned.
- [ ] The yearly tabletop: scenario, attendees, notes, and follow-up tickets.
- [ ] Evidence that staff know how to report a security concern, such as onboarding material or a posted address.

## What good looks like

A log with low-severity events in it too (a phishing email reported, a lost phone wiped), which shows the process is used. An empty log for a whole year makes auditors wonder whether events are being recorded.

## Common exceptions

- An incident handled in chat with no ticket, so it's missing from the population.
- No lessons learned recorded after a significant incident.
- The tabletop skipped, or held without notes.

The [incident report template](../templates/incident-report.md) covers what to record.
