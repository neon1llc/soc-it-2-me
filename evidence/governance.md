---
title: "Evidence: governance and risk"
description: "Proof that leadership oversees security, policies are approved and reviewed, and risks are assessed and owned."
---

Controls: GOV-01 to GOV-05, RISK-01 to RISK-04.

## What the auditor asks for

- [ ] An org chart naming the security owner, and their written responsibilities.
- [ ] Minutes or notes from leadership reviews of the security program, with date, attendees, and decisions.
- [ ] The policy set, each with version, owner, approver, and approval date.
- [ ] Records of the yearly policy review, even when nothing changed.
- [ ] Policy and code of conduct acknowledgments for everyone employed during the window.
- [ ] The risk assessment: method, register, scores, treatments, owners, and leadership sign-off on accepted risks.
- [ ] The system and data inventory, with owners and classifications.
- [ ] The report from the yearly independent review, and tickets closing its findings.
- [ ] The customer-facing description of commitments: trust page, security terms, or contract template.

## What good looks like

Leadership meeting notes with a security agenda item and a decision, not just "security discussed." A risk register with a review date in the window and named owners. Policies whose approval date falls before the window starts.

## Common exceptions

- Policies approved after the window started, so they weren't in effect for all of it.
- Acknowledgments missing for contractors, or for people hired late in the window.
- A risk assessment older than a year, or one that wasn't redone after a major change such as a new product.
