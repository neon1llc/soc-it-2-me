---
title: "Evidence: vendors and data"
description: "Proof that vendors are reviewed by risk, contracts carry security terms, vendor expectations are met, and customer data is classified and deleted on request."
---

Controls: VEN-01 to VEN-03, DATA-01, DATA-02.

## What the auditor asks for

- [ ] The vendor list with a risk tier for each, and which touch customer data.
- [ ] Reviews for new vendors before signing, and yearly reviews for high-risk vendors: SOC reports collected, questionnaires, and decisions.
- [ ] Contracts or data processing agreements with security and confidentiality terms for key vendors.
- [ ] The list of complementary user entity controls from each subservice organization's report, mapped to your own controls.
- [ ] The data classification policy, and the inventory showing each store's class.
- [ ] Customer deletion requests in the window, with completion dates.

## What good looks like

A vendor review form for each high-risk vendor, with the SOC report's period, its opinion, any exceptions noted, and the CUECs you're responsible for. The [vendor review template](../templates/vendor-review.md) has the form.

## Common exceptions

- A new tool adopted by a team, with customer data in it, never reviewed.
- Vendor SOC reports collected but never read, so their exceptions and CUECs were missed.
- Deletion requests completed late, or with no record.
