---
title: "Evidence: endpoints and offices"
description: "Proof that company devices are managed, encrypted, locked, and updated, that lost or retired devices are wiped, and that offices are secured."
---

Controls: END-01, END-02, PHY-01.

## What the auditor asks for

- [ ] The device inventory, matched to the staff list, so every person's devices are accounted for.
- [ ] A device management compliance report: encryption, screen lock, OS version, and endpoint protection, dated within the window.
- [ ] The policy for personal devices, if staff use them for work.
- [ ] A log of lost, stolen, and retired devices, with wipe confirmations.
- [ ] For offices: the access list, visitor log, and how keys or badges are removed at departure.
- [ ] Your cloud provider's SOC report, for the data center controls you inherit.

## What good looks like

A compliance report where non-compliant devices have a ticket and a fix date. A retired device log with serial numbers and wipe certificates.

## Common exceptions

- Devices outside management, such as a founder's laptop or a contractor's personal machine with production access.
- Disk encryption turned on, but no evidence it was on for the whole window.
- Retired laptops sitting in a drawer, unwiped and unlogged.
