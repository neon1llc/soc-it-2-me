---
title: "Evidence: access"
description: "Proof that access is approved, reviewed, removed on time, protected with strong sign-in, and limited for administrators."
---

Controls: ACC-01 to ACC-06.

## What the auditor asks for

- [ ] The list of in-scope systems, and who owns each.
- [ ] Access request tickets for the window (the population), and for a sample: requester, approver, date approved, date granted.
- [ ] A list of everyone who left during the window, with last day, and the time each account was disabled in every in-scope system.
- [ ] Quarterly access reviews: the dated user export, the reviewer's marks, the removal tickets, and the sign-off, for every quarter.
- [ ] Identity provider settings showing single sign-on and two-step sign-in enforced, plus a user report showing who's enrolled.
- [ ] The list of administrators and production access holders, and evidence it's reviewed.
- [ ] The secrets and service account inventory, with owners and last rotation dates.

## What good looks like

Departures matched to disable times in one table, every gap under a business day. Access reviews that show removals, because a review that never removes anything looks like a rubber stamp.

## Common exceptions

- A departed person's account left active in one tool outside single sign-on.
- A quarterly review skipped or done late, or one system left out of it.
- Two-step sign-in enforced for staff, but not for a break-glass or shared admin account.
- Access granted by an engineer to themselves with no approval.
