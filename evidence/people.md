---
title: "Evidence: people"
description: "Proof of background checks, confidentiality agreements, training, and a fair disciplinary process."
---

Controls: PPL-01 to PPL-04, plus the hiring half of GOV-04.

## What the auditor asks for

- [ ] A list of everyone hired during the window, with start dates (the population).
- [ ] For a sample of hires: background check completion before access was granted, where the law allows checks.
- [ ] Signed confidentiality agreements for the same sample, including contractors.
- [ ] Security awareness training completion for everyone, at hire and yearly, with dates.
- [ ] The disciplinary policy, and an anonymized log of security-related cases if any occurred.
- [ ] Job descriptions or role documents for key security roles.

## What good looks like

An HR system export that shows hire date, check completion date, agreement signed date, and training date side by side, so the auditor can see the order in one view.

## Common exceptions

- Access granted the day before the background check came back.
- Training completed weeks after the policy's deadline for new hires.
- Contractors left out of every people control because they weren't in the HR system.
