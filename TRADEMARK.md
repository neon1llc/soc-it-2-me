"SOC It 2 Me", "neon1", the neon1 wordmark and the neon1 logo belong to neon1 LLC. The open licenses in this repository cover the words, checklists and code. They don't cover the names or the logo.

**You're welcome to:**

- Say that your work is based on or adapted from SOC It 2 Me, with a link back. That's the whole point.
- Print and hand out unmodified pages, logo included, for workshops, classrooms, community groups and offices.
- Mention SOC It 2 Me and neon1 when you review it, teach from it or write about it.

**Please don't:**

- Use either name or the logo for a product, service, course or site in a way that suggests neon1 made it or endorses it.
- Hand out modified material under the SOC It 2 Me or neon1 name. If you change it, remove the logo, call it something else, and say it's adapted from SOC It 2 Me.

Not sure? Ask. The answer is usually yes.
